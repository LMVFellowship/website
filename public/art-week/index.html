Emerging Visual Artists

There isn’t a shortage of talent in Miami. There’s a shortage of access.

LMV is designed to shorten that distance. We’re building the bridge between making strong work and having the relationships, knowledge, visibility, and professional infrastructure needed to move a career forward.

Here’s a look at what we have planned

We’re turning Miami Art Week into a live classroom.

  1. Nov 29

    Sunday

    The Art of Storytelling

    We’re kicking things off at Tomorrowland with an interactive storytelling workshop led by NPR journalist Wilkine Brutus (followed by an R&B party).

  2. Dec 1

    Tuesday

    The Business of Art

    A hands-on workshop with Google exploring how artists can use Gemini to support the business side of their creative practice.

  3. Dec 2

    Wednesday

    Welcome Dinner

    Our official Miami Art Week welcome dinner, sponsored by Google, bringing together artists, partners, and supporters.

  4. Dec 3

    Thursday

    Cultural Day + Soul Basel

    A day exploring the history, art, and culture of Overtown, Brownsville, and Liberty City. We’ll close out the evening at Soul Basel in Overtown and also experience an exhibition honoring Overtown legend Purvis Young, curated and installed by one of our very own LMV artists.

  5. Dec 4

    Friday

    Art Week Experiences

    Studio visits, art fairs, exhibitions, and parties! A chance for our artists to experience Miami Art Week and connect with the broader creative community.

  6. Dec 5

    Saturday

    Closing Breakfast + Choose Your Own Adventure

    We’ll come together for breakfast before giving everyone the opportunity to explore the city and Art Week at their own pace.

  7. Dec 6

    Sunday

    A LONG exhale

    A moment to reflect on the week and prepare for the year ahead as we begin our 2027 fellowship journey.
